# Alternative machines

The supported first release targets Apple Silicon MacBooks and Windows 11 with WSL2 Ubuntu. Intel Macs, Windows without WSL2, older Windows, Chromebooks, mobile devices, and other Linux distributions may require different packages or hosted rendering.

Do not force the installer onto an incompatible computer. Contact the instructor through the course channel with your computer model, OS version, RAM, storage, and WSL2/virtualization availability. Never include keys, passwords, tokens, or private media.
