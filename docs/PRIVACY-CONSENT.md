# Privacy, consent, and commercial use

## What stays local

Project checkpoints, Whisper models/transcripts, time fitting, FFmpeg/Remotion rendering, and outputs remain local unless you upload or share them.

## Cloud boundaries

- Codex receives brief and creative context used in its session.
- Grok receives the still and animation prompt.
- Fish Audio receives dialogue and the selected Fish voice reference.
- ElevenLabs receives dialogue and its own voice ID only when selected or used as allowed fallback.

Use only voices, likenesses, products, music, footage, and scripts you own or have permission to use. Do not clone a real person's voice without documented authorization or create deceptive endorsements.

Before commercial publication, check current licenses. ElevenLabs Free output is non-commercial and requires attribution. Fish Audio's temporary free API may have restrictions. Preserve required Grok watermarks and provenance. Never switch providers to bypass moderation, consent, or licensing.
